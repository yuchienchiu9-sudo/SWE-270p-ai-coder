BudgetMaster helps users manage budgets, income, expenses, categories, and goals. Use Python and Streamlit with in-memory demo data.

Requirements:
1. Add income transactions with amount, category, and description.
2. Add expense transactions with positive amounts and categories.
3. Reject invalid or negative transaction amounts.
4. Set monthly budget limits for spending categories.
5. Calculate balances, category totals, and budget remaining.
6. Display spending summaries and at least one chart.
7. Provide tabs for Transactions, Budgets, and Reports.
